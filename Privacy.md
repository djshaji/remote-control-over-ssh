# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-05-25

This Privacy Policy applies to the **remote control over ssh** Android app (`org.acoustixaudio.opiqo.remotecontroloverssh`).

## Summary

This app is designed to let you connect to machines you configure over SSH and trigger commands from your Android device. The app does **not** require an account and does **not** include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Information the app stores

The app stores the information you enter or import to make the app work, including:

- SSH profile details such as host, port, username, and host fingerprint
- Imported private key files, which are copied into the app's internal storage
- Remote profile names and command mappings you configure for buttons and sliders

This information is stored locally on your device using the app's local database and internal app storage.

## How information is used

The app uses your stored information only to:

- show and manage your saved SSH and remote profiles
- establish SSH connections to servers you configure
- send the commands you mapped to remote controls

## Network use

The app uses the Android `INTERNET` permission only to connect directly to the SSH servers you choose. When you use the app, connection information and commands are transmitted to those servers as part of normal SSH operation.

The app developer does **not** operate a separate backend service that receives your SSH profiles or command mappings through the app.

## Sharing of data

The app does not sell your personal information and does not share your data with advertisers or analytics providers.

Your data may leave your device only in these cases:

- when the app connects directly to an SSH server you configured
- when Android system backup or device transfer features copy app data, depending on your device settings

## Data retention and deletion

Your saved profiles, command mappings, and imported key files remain on your device until you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Deleting SSH profiles may also remove associated stored key files used by the app.

## Security

The app stores data in app-local storage on your device. No method of electronic storage or transmission is guaranteed to be perfectly secure, so you should protect your device appropriately and only connect to systems you trust.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed to children under 13, and the app is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Updates will be reflected by changing the "Last updated" date in this file.
